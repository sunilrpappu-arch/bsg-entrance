Two Weeks to the
Entrance Exam

A study plan for our four divisional members, Young Lions and friends — built around 30 minutes on Monday, Wednesday and Friday evenings, so nobody has to choose between work and study. Five evenings to cover all twelve topics. Then the last week is yours.

Same link, every session meet.google.com/fmg-zxid-hip

Google Meet, 9:30–10:00 pm on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays. The link does not change and does not expire — the same one works for all eight evenings. Access is set to open: nobody has to ask to be let in, and anyone can join before the host arrives.

“Exert yourself in the two ways of practice and study.” Nobody here is being measured. We sit this exam to deepen conviction in our own faith and practice — that is the whole of it. Several of us are attempting it again, and attempting again is itself the practice. Come as you are, on whichever evenings you can.

How each 30 minutes runs

The shape is the same every evening, so nobody has to wonder what is coming. Three daimoku to open. Five quick questions from last time in the quiz app — not a test, just a warm-up together. Sixteen minutes on tonight's topics. A live round of five questions on what we just covered. One minute to name the homework and close.

Rotate who leads. Give each of the four divisional members one evening to present. Teaching a topic is the fastest way to own it — and it turns the group from a class into a study team.

If someone misses an evening, nothing is lost. Every topic maps exactly onto a topic in the quiz app, so they can catch up in their own time and rejoin without falling behind.
